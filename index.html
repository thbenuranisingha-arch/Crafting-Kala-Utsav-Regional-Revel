Representing Nature by Crochet

A wetland of crochet lotuses, turtles and catfish, brought to life with physics, robotics, magnetism and light.

Explore the Project See How We Made It

About Our Project

Our project shows the blend of a beautiful wetland with lotuses amid greenery, including aquatic creatures like the turtle and the catfish. In this traditional craft we have included some physics to make our project eye-catching and unique. We have added more wonders, our so-called robotics, magnetism and lighting, to beautify the wetland.

Our Experience

What We Learned

  • New designs and stitches
  • Emotional balance
  • Problem solving
  • Pride and satisfaction on seeing the finished project
  • Amazement at how balls of yarn became a tangible creation

We learned crochet in Class 8 and made thousands of hair accessories, bouquets and keychains. Our quarrels with friends were solved through crochet, teaching us problem solving.

While Making This Project

  • Arguments, fun and memories with friends, seniors and teachers
